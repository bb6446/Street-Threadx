## Overview

**STREET THREADX** is a high-performance streetwear e-commerce platform engineered for speed, sharp visual hierarchy, and operational control. It pairs a bold, digital-brutalist B2C storefront with a full-featured B2B admin dashboard powered by the Google Gemini API.

---

## Key Features

### Storefront (B2C)
- **Responsive Product Grid:** Fast, mobile-first catalog with dynamic category and color filtering.
- **URL Hash Deep Linking:** Shareable direct links to specific views, products, and filtered states.
- **Real-Time Cart:** Instant drawer updates with size and quantity controls.
- **Single-Page Checkout:** Streamlined checkout flow designed to minimize drop-off.
- **Customer Reviews & Support HQ:** Integrated product ratings/reviews and a dedicated customer support hub.

### Admin Dashboard (B2B)
- **Real-Time Analytics:** Live tracking for revenue, conversion rates, and Average Order Value (AOV) powered by Recharts.
- **Multi-Step Product Wizard:** Structured product creation with SKU and variant-level inventory tracking.
- **Bulk Operations & Alerts:** CSV bulk updates and smart low-stock alerts to prevent overselling.
- **Order Lifecycle Management:** End-to-end order status tracking, fulfillment controls, and history.

### AI & Security
- **Gemini AI Integration:** Automated generation of high-converting product descriptions and SEO metadata.
- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):** Granular permissions across **Super Admin**, **Editor**, and **Support** roles.
- **Authentication & Auditing:** Simulated Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) and global activity audit logs.
